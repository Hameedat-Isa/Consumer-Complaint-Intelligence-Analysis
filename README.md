# Consumer Complaint Trends and Response Intelligence Platform

**AnalystLab Africa | Experience Lab Internship Programme | Week 4**

## Project Overview

This project analyses 62,516 consumer complaints relating to Bank of America between May 2017 and August 2023, using complaint records from the Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB).

The objective is to develop a consumer complaint intelligence solution that helps stakeholders monitor complaint trends, identify frequently reported financial products and issues, and examine recorded company responses and complaint-forwarding intervals.

## Tools and Techniques

- Microsoft Power BI
- Power Query M for data cleaning and transformation
- DAX for KPI calculations
- Excel and analytical scripts for data validation
- Data visualisation and business intelligence

## Dashboard Pages

1. **Executive Complaint Overview:** Complaint totals, monthly trends, financial products, recorded response timeliness and average forwarding intervals.
2. **Product and Issue Analysis:** Financial products, sub-products, frequently reported consumer issues and product-versus-issue comparisons.
3. **Company Response Analysis:** Company response classifications, timely and untimely responses, in-progress complaints and public-response categories.
4. **Complaint Timeline Analysis:** Monthly and annual trends, forwarding distributions, product-level intervals and unusual forwarding delays.

## Key Findings

- Total complaints analysed: **62,516**
- Largest product category: **Checking or savings accounts (24,814 complaints)**
- Most frequently reported issue: **Managing an account (15,109 complaints)**
- Recorded timely-response rate: **96.06% of 61,022 eligible records**
- Excluded in-progress timeliness records: **1,494**
- Average CFPB complaint-forwarding interval: **1.22 days**
- Median CFPB complaint-forwarding interval: **0 days**

## Business Recommendations

1. Prioritise further investigation of recurring account-management complaints.
2. Monitor recorded response timeliness by product and reporting period.
3. Investigate unusually long CFPB complaint-forwarding intervals.

## Data Preparation and Validation

The analytical process included:

- Inspecting records and field types
- Checking duplicate complaint IDs
- Investigating missing values
- Validating date fields
- Standardising categorical values
- Creating calendar and forwarding-interval fields
- Validating KPI calculations against the source data

## Project Workflow

The Week 4 project was structured around sequential activities: data inspection, preparation, KPI development, dashboard development, validation, documentation and final presentation. This workflow supports reproducibility and reduces the risk of reporting inaccurate results.

## Limitations

The dataset represents recorded consumer complaints, not proven misconduct. CFPB forwarding intervals do not measure company resolution time. The data also covers only part of 2017 and 2023, so full-year comparisons require care.

## Data Source

Consumer Financial Protection Bureau (CFPB), Consumer Complaints on Financial Products and Services, Bank of America records, 2017–2023.
