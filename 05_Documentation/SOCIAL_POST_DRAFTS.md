## LinkedIn

Week 4 of the AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab focused on consumer complaint trends and response intelligence.

I worked with 62,516 Bank of America consumer complaint records (May 2017–August 2023), validated the source data, defined 12 KPI groups and documented a four-page complaint intelligence dashboard. The largest complaint category was checking or savings accounts (39.69%). Of the complaints with a recorded Yes/No response-timeliness value, 96.06% were marked timely; 1,494 in-progress records were correctly excluded.

The key learning was methodological: consumer-reported complaints are not proof of misconduct, and the time between CFPB receipt and forwarding is not company resolution time. My project schedule reserved an explicit validation and handover phase before the Thursday submission deadline, because sound reporting depends on checking definitions before drawing conclusions.
